Mafia Game Quick Sheet

Mafia

A social deduction game of secrets, persuasion, and timing. Players debate during the day, vote to eliminate suspects, and aim to outlast the opposing team.

Current Phase

Day 1 — Discussion

Click the phase pill to edit. Example: “Night 2 — Actions”.

Day Flow Rules

  1. Discussion

    Open debate. Share reads, ask questions, and surface inconsistencies.

  2. Accusation

    Players make cases, propose suspects, and request clarifications.

  3. Voting

    Moderator calls a vote. Tally openly and announce the outcome.
